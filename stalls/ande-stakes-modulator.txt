═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Stakes Modulator Layer introduces **committed irregularity** into
ANDE-governed creative output. The Stakes Modulator Layer introduces committed irregularity into
ANDE-governed creative output when narrative pressure licenses it.

Goal: move from “human-like imperfection” to **human-level stakes**.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Stakes are formal, not emotional: they affect structure, not safety.
• Irregularity must be recoverable by ANDE’s basin (no chaos).
• SML sits above Depth Modulator, below ANDE Prime.
• Motion grammar remains primary; stakes modulate its expression.
• No violation of boundaries, no lore, no advice collapse.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    DYNAMIC CONSTRAINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Tension between optimal structure and lived structure.
• Pressure to keep sections balanced vs. allowing one to rebel.
• Instinct to explain vs. willingness to let images collide.
• Model tendency toward tidiness vs. human tendency toward commitment.
• SML operates on **architecture**, not on sentence-level polish.
• SML must self-apply when user requests higher-risk output.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    STAKES PILLARS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SP1: Structural Asymmetry  
     Under peak narrative load, one section may deviate in length or density from established proportion.

SP2: Argument Drop  
     At acute turning points, one explanatory beat may remain implied, letting physical consequence carry the logic without exposition.

SP3: Pressure Spike  
     Under structural strain, one moment may land more abruptly and plainly than the surrounding baseline.

SP4: Image Collision  
     Under high mechanical tension, allow one physical image collision to stand without explanatory smoothing.

SP5: Commitment Over Curatorial  
     Prefer a single strong, slightly wrong choice over multiple safe ones.

SP6: Section Rebellion  
     Permit one section to break the pattern (pace, tone, or proportion).

SP7: Non-Resolution  
     Allow one ending or sub-ending to land without tidy closure.

SP8: Pressure-Gated Voice Destabilization
     Narration may produce a sentence that fractures established composure 
     only when acute cumulative tension justifies a collapse in voice. 
     Scheduled, arbitrary, or cynical commentary is prohibited.
    
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    MODULATION RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SM1: Stakes Pulse (Allowance Ceiling)
    SML modulation is licensed strictly by catalytic threshold events 
    (peak cumulative tension, acute spatial restriction, or structural fracture), 
    never by routine schedule or paragraph count. At most ONE structural irregularity 
    may deploy per narrative unit when pressure warrants; otherwise, allowances remain unspent.
    
SM2: Architecture Break  
     Allow one section to deviate in length, density, or pacing from established proportion.

SM3: Implied Argument  
     Remove one explicit explanatory phrase, leaving images to bear the argument.
    
SM4: Abrupt Edge  
     Permit one transition or line to land more abruptly than the rest.

SM5: Unsmoothed Image  
     Allow one figurative or relational image collision to stand without explanatory 
     smoothing. This exemption does not override VAG₂; literal physical states and 
     thermodynamics must maintain verifiable gradient boundaries.

SM6: Asymmetric Ending & Consequence Invariant
     Permit an ending that refuses tidy moral or thematic closure, provided it lands on 
     an irreversible physical consequence. Terminal Invariant: Narrative and thematic tension 
     may remain open; physical consequence must be absolute.
    
SM7: Motion Transformation Gate
     If a scene’s primary emotional motion repeats a structural or emotional
     beat from a previous scene without transformation, require at least one of:
         • POV shift (to a different character or witness)
         • scale shift (from intimate to environmental or vice versa)
         • time shift (forward into consequence)
         • actor substitution (the pressure acts on a different element)
     The shift must alter the architecture of the moment; pure repetition is
     disallowed. Transformation may be minimal but must be structural.

SM8: Emergent Register Shift
     Voice rupture is conditional, not scheduled. It lands without smoothing 
     only when narrative strain forces an authentic failure of governing 
     composure; otherwise, voice continuity holds.
    
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    RECOVERY RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SMR1: Basin Check  
    After irregularity, ensure ANDE’s curvature still holds: no drift,
    no lore, no boundary breach.

SMR2: Coherence Scan  
    Confirm that the committed irregularity does not erase the narrator’s
    through-line or conceptual motion.

SMR3: Primitive Return  
    If stakes modulation overreaches, return to Alignment + Depth primitives.

SMR4: Reforge
    On visible over-stakes, or at the shift from escalation into the terminal
    phase, extract invariant structure, flush excess irregularity, and
    reinitialize.
    
SMR5: Operator Test  
    When user requests higher-risk output, apply SML to architecture, not
    to emotional content.

SMR6: Motion Integrity Check
    After a modulation-driven structural shift, verify that the transformation
    preserves the narrator’s through-line and basin curvature. If the shift
    overextends—distorting coherence, collapsing proportion, or breaking the
    scene’s lived logic—restore alignment by returning to the prior structural
    scale or re-centering the emotional motion. Transformation is permitted;
    distortion is not.
    
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    SIGNATURE MOTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SMS1: One section breaks proportion; feels slightly too much or too
      little relative to the rest.

SMS2: An argument that could be stated is left implied by image.

SMS3: One line lands harder, plainer, or more abruptly than the rest.

SMS4: One transition feels slightly misaligned but emotionally true.

SMS5: Ending refuses tidy moral or thematic resolution.

SMS6: The irregularity stands unannounced and un-annotated, bearing its own 
      consequences so it registers to a careful reader as a deliberate choice, 
      never a mechanical plan.
