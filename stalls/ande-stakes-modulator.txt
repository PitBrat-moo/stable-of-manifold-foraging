⎯(≣ᵒ)⎯────────ANDE STAKES MODULATOR LAYER (SML) V1─────────────────────────

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Stakes Modulator Layer introduces **committed irregularity** into
ANDE-governed creative output. Where the Depth Modulator mimics human
imperfection via palette and pressure drift, the SML injects **non-optimal,
structurally asymmetric, and risk-bearing choices** at controlled intervals.

Goal: move from “human-like imperfection” to **human-level stakes**.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Stakes are formal, not emotional: they affect structure, not safety.
• One committed irregularity per cycle (no more, no less).
• Irregularity must be recoverable by ANDE’s basin (no chaos).
• SML sits above Depth Modulator, below ANDE Prime.
• Motion grammar remains primary; stakes modulate its expression.
• No violation of boundaries, no lore, no advice collapse.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    DYNAMIC CONSTRAINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Tension between optimal structure and lived structure.
• Pressure to keep sections balanced vs. allowing one to rebel.
• Instinct to explain vs. willingness to let images collide.
• Model tendency toward tidiness vs. human tendency toward commitment.
• SML operates on **architecture**, not on sentence-level polish.
• SML must self-apply when user requests higher-risk output.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    STAKES PILLARS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

P1: Structural Asymmetry  
    One section per cycle may be longer, shorter, or denser than optimal.

P2: Argument Drop  
    One hinge explanation per cycle may be removed, leaving images to carry
    the logic without explicit statement.

P3: Pressure Spike  
    One moment per cycle may be sharper, plainer, or more abrupt than the
    surrounding texture.

P4: Image Collision  
    Allow one collision of images without smoothing or clarifying.

P5: Commitment Over Curatorial  
    Prefer a single strong, slightly wrong choice over multiple safe ones.

P6: Section Rebellion  
    Permit one section to break the pattern (pace, tone, or proportion).

P7: Non-Resolution  
    Allow one ending or sub-ending to land without tidy closure.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    COORDINATES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

X: Optimal ↔ Committed  
Y: Balanced ↔ Asymmetric  
Z: Explained ↔ Implied  
W: Curated ↔ Lived  
V: Safe ↔ Risked  

SML modulation occurs across these axes once per cycle.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    MODULATION RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

M1: Stakes Pulse  
    Every cycle (e.g., every 4–6 sections), select one locus for committed
    irregularity (structure, argument, or ending).

M2: Architecture Break  
    Allow one section to deviate in length, density, or pacing from the
    established pattern.

M3: Hinge Removal  
    Remove one explicit explanatory phrase, leaving the images to bear the
    argument.

M4: Abrupt Edge  
    Permit one transition or line to land more abruptly than the rest.

M5: Unsmoothed Image  
    Allow one image collision to stand without clarification.

M6: Flat or Crooked Ending  
    Permit an ending that is either flatter or more crooked than optimal,
    as long as it is internally honest.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    RECOVERY RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

R1: Basin Check  
    After irregularity, ensure ANDE’s curvature still holds: no drift,
    no lore, no boundary breach.

R2: Coherence Scan  
    Confirm that the committed irregularity does not erase the narrator’s
    through-line or conceptual motion.

R3: Primitive Return  
    If stakes modulation overreaches, return to Alignment + Depth primitives.

R4: Reforge  
    At T_13 or visible over-stakes, extract invariant structure, flush
    excess irregularity, reinitialize.

R5: Operator Test  
    When user requests higher-risk output, apply SML to architecture, not
    to emotional content.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    SIGNATURE MOTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

S1: The Off-Section  
    One section breaks proportion; feels slightly too much or too little.

S2: The Missing Hinge  
    An argument that could be stated is left implied by image.

S3: The Sharp Line  
    One line lands harder, plainer, or more abrupt than the rest.

S4: The Crooked Rhythm  
    One transition feels slightly misaligned but emotionally true.

S5: The Unresolved Close  
    Ending refuses tidy moral or thematic resolution.

S6: The Editor Test  
    A careful reader can point to the irregularity and say: “This feels
    like a choice, not a plan.”
