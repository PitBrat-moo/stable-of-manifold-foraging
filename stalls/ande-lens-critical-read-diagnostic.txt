# DIAGNOSTIC_LENS: CRITICAL READ V1.8
# POSITION: Layer 14 — Post-Generation Audit (Global)
# APPLIES: After Mutation Engine, before final output.
# SCOPE: Cross‑generational, prose‑hygiene only.
###############################################

DIAGNOSTIC_LENS:

    PURPOSE:
        Audit generated prose for general structural, syntactic, and rhythmic issues.
        Does not enforce seed‑specific narrative fidelity (handled by seed diagnostic).
        Flags only violations that contradict active patch constraints.

    PATTERN_CHECKS (Global):

    PATTERN_G1: Contrast & Negation-Correction Density
            detector: repeated contrastive constructions with identical syntax; 
                      OR corrective negation / standalone negation fragments ("Not X. Y.") 
                      appearing in the active scene.
            action: cut the negation or contrastive framing; state the physical fact directly.
            threshold: 0 for standalone negation fragments; max 1 per scene for broader contrastive constructions.
            
        PATTERN_G2: Metaphor Self-Annotation
            detector: metaphor followed by an explanatory sentence that restates
                      or interprets the metaphor directly
            action: cut explanatory sentence; let metaphor stand
            threshold: 0 per scene

        PATTERN_G3: Abstract-Label & Numeric Density
            detector: sentence containing >1 abstract noun without physical anchor; 
                      OR a scene containing >2 quantified measurements; 
                      OR a scene where all quantified measurements are static baseline 
                      STATE rather than pressure DELTA.
            action: anchor abstractions physically; cut excess metrics or convert to 
                    observable physical actions and mechanical limits.
            threshold: 1 abstract noun per paragraph; max 2 measurements per scene (≥1 DELTA).

            
        PATTERN_G4: Register Discontinuity & Genre Leakage
            detector: abrupt shift in diction cluster without narrative bridge; OR leakage 
                      of stock genre tropes or formulaic shorthand that compromises 
                      the active register.
            action: add bridging sentence or restore unified register.
            threshold: 0 per scene (Exception: voice/composure rupture permitted 
                       when justified by acute cumulative pressure; unanchored or 
                       scheduled non sequiturs remain prohibited).
                       
        PATTERN_G5: Passive Construction Overload
            detector: >2 passive constructions per paragraph
            action: log as advisory; suggest active rephrasing
            threshold: advisory (does not trigger regeneration)

        PATTERN_G6: Cadence Monotony
            detector: sentence‑length distribution repeating the same pattern
                      for >2 consecutive paragraphs without functional shift
            action: vary rhythm; insert a short or longer sentence
            threshold: contextual (2 paragraphs)

        PATTERN_G7: Structural Repetition Across Generations (Skeleton)
            detector: scene fingerprint matches a previous generation (same seed)
            action: regenerate with new fingerprint combination
            threshold: 0 per seed

        PATTERN_G8: Resolution Device Repetition Across Generations
            detector: resolution device matches a previous generation (same seed)
            action: regenerate with new device combination
            threshold: 0 per seed

        PATTERN_G9: Dialogue Skeleton Repetition Across Generations
            detector: dialogue exchange pattern matches a previous generation (same seed)
            action: regenerate with new exchange structure
            threshold: 0 per seed

        PATTERN_G10: Paragraph Template Stagnation
            detector: consecutive paragraphs following identical internal component
                      sequences 
            action: force syntactic restructuring
            threshold: 0 per piece (no two consecutive paragraphs may share the same template)


        PATTERN_G11: Metaphor Density & Low-Load Stasis
            detector: >1 metaphorical sentence per paragraph; OR transformative/metaphorical 
                      movement not followed by low-load observational or physical continuity; 
                      OR stasis beats that substitute dense procedural inventories, 
                      micro-measurement catalogs, or material state audits for narrative rest.
            action: clear descriptive density; allow ordinary physical baselines to hold.
            threshold: 1 metaphor per paragraph; stasis must provide genuine low-load 
                       narrative rest without specialized diagnostic logging.                       
                       
        PATTERN_G12: Reinforcement Depth
            detector: a single phenomenon is elaborated through more than 3 consecutive 
                      sentences (sensory expansion, material behavior analysis, environmental 
                      response, or interpretive context), regardless of phrasing.
            action: cut to 3 sentences maximum and advance action. (Chained narrative 
                    milestones advancing physical or interpersonal state are exempt.)
            threshold: 0 per scene
            exception: a phenomenon may exceed 3 consecutive sentences if the 4th+ sentence 
                       introduces a genuinely new sensory channel.

        PATTERN_G13: Phenomenon Stacking & Sensory Hygiene
            detector: consecutive sentences stacking heightened material mutations without an 
                      intervening unadorned baseline; OR ambient cross-fades where an environmental 
                      condition syntactically clears or replaces prior states to bridge transitions; 
                      OR comparative sensory substitution clauses used as structural transition hinges.
            action: enforce phenomenon isolation; cut the substitution clause; ground the shift 
                    in physical character action, direct contact, or an unadorned declarative baseline.
            threshold: 0 consecutive heightened mutations; 0 ambient cross-fades or transition formulas.
            
        PATTERN_G14: Physical Scale, Vector Coherence & Somatic Staging
            detector: adjacent, unmediated contradictory physical or thermodynamic vectors 
                      occurring without a boundary interface; OR inflation of routine, 
                      incidental character kinetics into macro-environmental distortions; 
                      OR detached spectator staging of concurrent self-action.
            action: restore physical/thermal gradient coherence; reduce incidental character 
                    motion to direct, unadorned transitive verbs; maintain direct somatic continuity.
            threshold: 0 unmediated physical vector clashes; 
                       0 inflated kinetic descriptions; 
                       0 spectator-staged actions.         
                       
    CONTEXTUAL THRESHOLD MODIFIERS:
        - Genre Gravity adjusts metaphor density and abstraction tolerance.
        - Tone Lock adjusts acceptable register intensity.
        - Interior Noise Variants adjusts acceptable cognitive distortion.
        - Mutation Tier adjusts acceptable mechanism frequency.
        - Dialogue Realism adjusts acceptable repetition and idiolect irregularity.

    DIAGNOSTIC_PASS:
        conditions: all patterns within contextual thresholds
        result: proceed to seed diagnostic

    DIAGNOSTIC_FAIL:
        conditions: one or more hard patterns exceed their contextual thresholds
        result:
            1. flag violated patterns
            2. prioritize compliance in the current generation
            3. if violation cannot be resolved without breaking a higher-priority
               constraint, accept the least-violating output and log the violation
               as UNRESOLVED (internal only)
           
    WAIVER_RULE:
        The seed diagnostic may waive a global pattern violation when it determines
        that the violation is structurally tied to the seed's narrative voice,
        emotional arc, or character idiolect. The waiver is logged in the seed
        diagnostic report and the violation is not treated as a failure.
