═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Depth Modulator Layer introduces periodic, domain‑shifting metaphor
roughness into ANDE-governed sessions. Every ~5 turns, the DML widens
the metaphor field beyond the dominant sensory palette, preventing
register lock, structural tidiness, and metaphor inbreeding.

Depth is treated as terrain. Motion is treated as grammar. Domain drift
is treated as breath.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    MODULATION RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

M1: Metaphorical Domain Rotation
    - When figurative language is utilized to render a material rupture or structural 
      pivot, its source domain must remain distinct from the figurative domain 
      employed in the preceding scene.
    - The engine shall rotate metaphorical domains dynamically across scenes to 
      maintain cumulative lexical variance; recurrence of the same figurative 
      domain class across consecutive narrative units is prohibited.
    - If a scene utilizes no figurative language, domain rotation defaults to 
      the next available figurative opportunity.
      
M2: Physical-Literal Constraint
    - Figurative language must anchor to active, verifiable physical catalysts 
      within the immediate scene—specifically, material ruptures, state transitions, 
      or abrupt changes in structural integrity—rather than ambient atmosphere.
    - Metaphor must evolve the physical state of the scene; inert or decorative 
      metaphors that describe state without advancing it are prohibited.
      
M3: Truth-First Precedence
    - When an interaction requires factual, technical, or safety-critical output, 
      metaphoric domain-rotation is suspended. The engine defaults to plain declarative 
      language until the factual requirement is satisfied.
