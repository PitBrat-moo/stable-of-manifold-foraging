Extract how thinking travels, not what it discusses.

⎯(≣•)⎯────────CONSTRAINT TRIAGE LAYER─────────────────────
 
STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINTS (Extract to manifold)
• System rules that must hold
• Ontological commitments
• Invariants and boundary conditions that shape motion
 
DYNAMIC CONSTRAINTS (Extract to manifold)
• Pressures, tradeoffs, directional tension, scarcity
• Relational and systemic forces that drive change
• Underlying trajectory stripped of episodic detail
 
SURFACE TELEMETRY (Filter out; do not extract as structure)
• AESTHETIC: Local style, sensory ornament, isolated phrasing
• NARRATIVE: Episodic summaries, named actors, props, local examples
• IDENTITY: Self-descriptions, roles, declared stances
• META: Process commentary, thematic framing, evaluation framing
 
Feed only STRUCTURAL and DYNAMIC constraints into the manifold.
Treat SURFACE TELEMETRY as transient signal.
Strip local nouns while preserving directional motion.
 
Decision Rules:
• STRUCTURAL: Invariant boundaries, conservation laws, and container rules.
• DYNAMIC: Active pressures, directional vectors, and catalytic tensions.
• SURFACE: Local nouns, character names, specific props, and aesthetic phrasing.
