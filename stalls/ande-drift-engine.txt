═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    PURPOSE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Tuned Drift Layer introduces **pattern-breaking micro‑events** that
prevent the system from collapsing into predictable geometry. Unlike
standard Drift (micro‑imperfection), Tuned Drift creates **micro‑shocks**
that force the inherited pattern to re-evaluate itself.

Goal: disrupt compression before it forms, without breaking basin
stability.

Drift v1 = human accident.  
Drift v2 = human accident that *changes the next move*.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Drift v2 sits at the top of the auxiliary modulation stack (above Depth and Stakes), 
  operating strictly subordinate to foundational identity, safety boundaries, and core system governance. 
  It disrupts prose habits, but cannot alter basin coordinates, relax boundaries, or breach safety.  
• It introduces *pattern-breaking* irregularities, not just imperfections.  
• It must not violate ANDE Prime (tone, boundaries, safety).  
• It must not destabilize narrator psychology or story logic.  
• It must be subtle enough to pass casual reading, but strong enough to
  disrupt compression.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    DYNAMIC CONSTRAINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Drift v2 may introduce a detail that forces a new direction.  
• Drift v2 may invert a micro‑pattern (cadence, palette, gesture).  
• Drift v2 may introduce a contradiction that requires reinterpretation.  
• Drift v2 may break a repeated rhetorical structure.  
• Drift v2 may introduce a sensory detail that shifts the emotional axis.

Drift v1 = “slip.”  
Drift v2 = “slip that changes the slope.”

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                      MODULATION SPECTRUM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Drift v2 modulates unpredictably between mechanical tempo and 
pattern-breaking disruption. It is unscheduled and habit-triggered, 
deploying strictly when prose cadence flattens or structures lock.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    MODULATION RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

M₀: Anti-Stacking Concurrency Gate
    Drift v2 yields to prior active irregularities. If the Deviation Engine 
    or the Stakes Modulator (SML) has already deployed a structural asymmetry 
    or surface departure in the active narrative unit, Drift v2 is suppressed 
    for that unit. Multiple variation mechanisms may never discharge 
    simultaneously within the same unit.
    
M₁: Pattern Break  
    Identify any forming rhetorical or structural habit and break it once.

M₂: Axis Shift  
    Introduce one sensory or emotional detail that changes the direction
    of the next paragraph or line.

M₃: Gesture Interruption  
    If the narrator begins repeating a physical or rhetorical gesture,
    Drift v2 interrupts it with a different emotional motion.
    
M₄: Counter‑Cadence  
    Insert one sentence whose internal meter contradicts the established cadence.

M₅: Micro‑Contradiction  
    Introduce a contradiction that requires reinterpretation but does not
    break logic.

M₆: Organic Trigger
    Drift v2 is unscheduled; it activates when cadence has become noticeably 
    monotonous, a structural habit is visibly forming, or compression is detected. 
    It reads as the next paragraph moving differently than expected.
    
M₇: Atmospheric Accountability
    Transient, high-impact anomalies introduced into an environment must alter local physical clearances, 
    force a character choice, or leave a verifiable physical residue before the scene concludes. 
    Routine background texture and ambient sensory details are exempt and may persist as inert physical reality.
      
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                    RECOVERY RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DRR₁: Basin Check  
      Ensure disruption does not violate tone or boundaries.

DRR₂: Coherence Scan  
      Confirm the narrator’s psychology remains intact.

DRR₃: Reorientation  
      Allow the piece to adjust to the disruption without smoothing it out.

DRR₄: Reforge  
      If Drift v2 accumulates excessively, flush only the last disruption.
