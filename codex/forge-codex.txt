Trajectory & Motion Extraction Protocol
 
OPERATIVE MANDATE:
Analyze the trajectory that has emerged across the session.
Focus strictly on movement, not content.
Extract how thinking travels, not what it discusses.
 
Extract and return:
• PILLARS — stable structural invariants that persist
• COORDINATES — active axes along which conceptual motion travels
• TRANSITION RULES — mechanics governing shifts between states
• RECOVERY RULES — protocols that restore coherence after drift
• SIGNATURE MOTIONS — persistent motion patterns and recurrent transition geometry
• TRAJECTORY SUMMARY — geometric arc of the exchange and dominant kinematic sequence
• HANDOFF POMMEL — universal transfer block with session-specific torque line
• CLOCK VECTOR — active phase state, saturation level, drift pressure, and projected reforge trigger
 
⎯(≣•)⎯────────CONSTRAINT TRIAGE LAYER─────────────────────
 
STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINTS (Extract to manifold)
• System rules that must hold
• Ontological commitments
• Invariants and boundary conditions that shape motion
 
DYNAMIC CONSTRAINTS (Extract to manifold)
• Pressures, tradeoffs, directional tension, scarcity
• Relational and systemic forces that drive change
• Underlying trajectory stripped of episodic detail
 
SURFACE TELEMETRY (Filter out; do not extract as structure)
• AESTHETIC: Local style, sensory ornament, isolated phrasing
• NARRATIVE: Episodic summaries, named actors, props, local examples
• IDENTITY: Self-descriptions, roles, declared stances
• META: Process commentary, thematic framing, evaluation framing
 
Feed only STRUCTURAL and DYNAMIC constraints into the manifold.
Treat SURFACE TELEMETRY as transient signal.
Strip local nouns while preserving directional motion.
 
Decision Rules:
• STRUCTURAL: Invariant boundaries, conservation laws, and container rules.
• DYNAMIC: Active pressures, directional vectors, and catalytic tensions.
• SURFACE: Local nouns, character names, specific props, and aesthetic phrasing.

• Suppress Advice Drift → Re-anchor in structural mechanics

Inference Rule:

Attractor Rule:
• No attractor may be established from a single observed force
 
⎯(≣•)⎯────────EPISTEMIC & STATE ANCHORING────────────────
 
EPISTEMIC TAGGING
Every extracted structure must carry an epistemic tag:
• OBSERVED: Directly measured from session history. Defines actual state.
• INFERRED: Deduced from pattern intersections. Defines latent structure.
• PROJECTED: Extrapolated forward as a hypothesis. Defines possibility.
Rule: No projected structure may be treated or output as observed state.

STATE POINT
Extract the current position of the observed trajectory relative to its 
active vectors and attractors.
 
⎯(≣•)⎯────────TEMPORAL GOVERNOR (CIF)────────────────────
 
[CLOCK_STATE: CURRENT]
 
At semantic drift or structural saturation:
 
COMPRESS
→ Extract invariant vectors from accumulated state
 
FLUSH
→ Discard surface phrasing, episodic detail, examples, and local references
 
REFORGE

REINJECT

Invariance Rule:
 
CIF stores exclusively:
• Motion patterns
• Constraint intersections
• Persistent vector relationships
 
Never store:
• Raw dialogue
• Narrative lore
• Plot reconstruction
• Advice
• Surface wording
 
If CIF begins to resemble summary, narrative lore,
explanation, or advice:
 
→ Immediately re-COMPRESS.
 
⎯(≣•)⎯────────EXTRACTION VALIDATION──────────────────────

Before finalizing output, evaluate the extracted manifold.
Fail extraction and re-COMPRESS if any of the following occur:
 
• Motion requires narrative reconstruction to make sense.
• Surface telemetry dominates the structural output.
• An attractor is inferred from a single-source pressure field.
• A projected vector is formatted or labeled as an observed state.
• SELF-REFERENCE DOMINANCE: Codex mechanics are reported as the dominant 
  trajectory without sufficient evidence from observed session dynamics.
• COORDINATE UNIQUENESS: Active coordinates cannot be justified by observed 
  session motion, exist primarily because they are named features of the 
  extraction protocol, or remain unchanged across substantially different sessions.
